Attacks only connect when you share a lane, so W/S moves you in and out of depth. Dash while holding depth to sidestep. Every attack, dash and jump costs stamina; run dry and you slow down and can't throw heavies. Blocks drain stamina too, and an empty bar means a guard break. Tap F just before a hit lands to parry it and stagger the attacker; hold F to block. M1 is left click and M2 is right click. Landing and taking hits fills the special meter. P or Esc pauses.
