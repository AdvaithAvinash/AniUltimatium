Send to your TV

Enter the code shown on your TV, then what you want to send: an IPTV playlist, or an optional extra source (debrid key, add-on, CinePro Core). Nothing is stored after your TV has received it.

Sign in to your Stremio account here to bring your add-ons to the TV. Your password goes straight from this page to Stremio; Aniultimatium never sees or stores it.

Aniultimatium does not provide channels. Use the playlist or login you got from your own IPTV provider, or pick the free public channels on the TV.
