AniUltimatium Lite

AniUltimatium Lite is a sub-project of AniUltimatium: a separate Samsung TV app with the same remote-friendly design that plays only what you add — your own Stremio add-ons, debrid account, IPTV playlist and direct streams. It comes with no content source built in.

AniUltimatium or AniUltimatium Lite?

AniUltimatium: anime, movies and TV shows with built-in sources, plus IPTV. Samsung and Android TV.
AniUltimatium Lite: nothing built in; you choose every source. Samsung TVs.

Set up Lite

  1. Download AniultimatiumLite.wgt from the latest release and install it with Apps2Samsung.
  2. Follow the short navigation tutorial on first start (Settings → Tips shows it again).
  3. Settings → Extra sources → Add-ons → Send from phone: scan the QR code and sign in to Stremio to bring your add-ons, or add one by address.
  4. Optionally add a debrid account, an IPTV playlist (Live TV) or a direct stream (Direct stream page).

Frequently asked questions

What is AniUltimatium Lite?

AniUltimatium Lite is a separate Samsung Tizen TV app and a sub-project of AniUltimatium. It uses the same remote-friendly interface but ships no content source of its own: it only plays what you add yourself.

How is Lite different from AniUltimatium?

AniUltimatium includes built-in anime sources and hosted movie players. Lite includes none of them: no scrapers, no hosted players, no built-in playlists or channel lists. You bring your own Stremio add-ons, debrid account, IPTV playlist or direct stream addresses.

Which add-ons come preinstalled in Lite?

Only add-ons that give information, never video: Cinemeta (titles and details), WatchHub (links to where a title streams legally), OpenSubtitles v3 and SubMaker (subtitles). You can switch each one off or remove it.

Can I use my Stremio account?

Yes. Scan the QR code in Settings, sign in to your Stremio account on your phone (your password goes straight from the phone to Stremio, never to our server) and choose the add-ons to bring to the TV. You can also manage add-ons on the TV.

Does Lite support debrid and P2P?

Yes: Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize and TorBox. Peer-to-peer results from your own add-ons play through your debrid account or your own streaming server (for example the Stremio service on a computer at home). P2P is on by default and can be switched off in Settings.

Does Lite play my own streams?

Yes. The Direct stream page plays any .m3u8 or .mp4 address you enter or send from your phone, and Live TV plays your own M3U or Xtream IPTV playlist.

Which TVs does Lite support?

Samsung Tizen TVs (2018, Tizen 4.0, and newer). Lite is installed as its own app, so it never replaces AniUltimatium on the same TV.
